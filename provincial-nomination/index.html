How provincial nomination works

Provinces and territories select candidates who meet local economic needs and intend to settle there. A nomination is followed by a federal permanent-residence application; IRCC makes the final decision. Express Entry and non-Express Entry routes have different requirements and procedures.

An EOI registration, an invitation and a nomination are different stages. Provincial scores are not interchangeable, and none is the federal CRS score.

A separate regional pathway

Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP)

AIP covers New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador. It is an employer-driven immigration program, not a Provincial Nominee Program. A qualifying offer from a designated employer, settlement arrangements and provincial endorsement are central steps before applying to IRCC.

Federal program availability does not remove provincial restrictions. Employer designation, endorsement intake, occupation rules and allocations must be checked for the relevant province. For example, New Brunswick has paused new employer designation applications for the remainder of 2026.
