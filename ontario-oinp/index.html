The current Ontario route

Ontario replaced its former streams with the Workforce Priority stream in 2026. Its EOI portal opened on August 4, 2026. Older Foreign Worker, International Student, Masters Graduate or PhD Graduate scoring tables should not be used for a new Workforce Priority EOI.

This page and calculator cover job-offer applicants in TEER 0–5. Eligibility differs between the skilled-occupation and other-occupation pathways, even where the EOI scoring factors are shared. Self-employed physicians require a different assessment and should not use this calculator.

From job offer to permanent residence

  1. Employer and job review. Confirm the NOC, workplace, wage and employer eligibility; the employer completes the required portal steps.
  2. EOI registration. Check the applicable pathway and support every claimed point with evidence. Registration does not guarantee selection.
  3. Invitation and application. The employer and applicant must complete their respective steps within the applicable deadlines.
  4. Nomination and federal application. Follow the applicable Express Entry or non-Express Entry route; federal eligibility and admissibility remain separate requirements.
