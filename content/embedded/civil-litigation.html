Understand the Dispute and Your Options

A dispute can interrupt business operations, delay payment or put an important relationship at risk. CanWise Law helps individuals and businesses assess the legal issues and decide how to respond.

We begin with the contract, the relevant communications and records, and your objectives. We explain the available options, the evidence needed and the likely procedural steps so you can weigh the cost, timing and risks of proceeding. Advice and communication are available in English and Mandarin.

Contract & Commercial Disputes

Contract Disputes

Advice on disputes about contractual performance, payment, breach or termination, including commercial contracts and service agreements. We examine the terms, the parties’ conduct and the records supporting each position.

Commercial Disputes

Assistance with disagreements arising from business relationships and transactions. We review the transaction documents, correspondence and financial records relevant to the dispute, and identify the legal issues and practical priorities.

How We Approach a Dispute

  1. Initial Assessment

    We review the key documents, chronology and your desired outcome, identify material gaps in the evidence and consider applicable deadlines. We discuss the strengths, weaknesses and practical consequences of the available approaches.

  2. Negotiation & Settlement

    Where appropriate, we prepare or respond to demand letters, communicate with the other party or their lawyer, and negotiate settlement terms. We help you assess proposals and document an agreed resolution.

  3. Litigation & Representation

    Where court proceedings are appropriate, we discuss commencing or defending a claim and the procedural work involved. Representation may include pleadings, evidence preparation, motions, settlement discussions and hearings, depending on the matter and agreed retainer.

  4. Scope, Cost & Next Steps

    Before taking on the work, we confirm the services, fee arrangement and responsibilities in a written retainer. We revisit strategy as the evidence and proceedings develop, and explain decisions that require your instructions.

Preparing for a Discussion

Bring the agreement and amendments, important correspondence, invoices or payment records, and any court documents you have received. A short chronology and a clear description of your desired outcome help focus the initial review.

The appropriate strategy and scope of representation depend on the facts, evidence and stage of the dispute. Settlement and litigation outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
