Important fee information: All amounts are professional fees in Canadian dollars. HST, court filing fees, process-server fees, experts, valuations, transcripts, travel, courier charges, and other disbursements are additional. Fixed fees apply only to the stated standard scope and may change if a matter becomes contested or requires additional work. Each party should obtain independent legal advice where appropriate. Ontario law does not permit contingency fees for family law matters. Final fees and services are confirmed in a written retainer agreement. No outcome is guaranteed.
