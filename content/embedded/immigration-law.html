When Immigration Requires Legal Strategy

Canadian immigration applicants may choose to work with a licensed immigration consultant or a lawyer. Our practice is particularly focused on matters that require legal analysis, complex written submissions, or representation in immigration litigation.

Many of our clients come to us after a previous refusal or with circumstances that make a straightforward application more difficult. We regularly handle complex temporary and permanent residence applications, Federal Court judicial reviews and mandamus proceedings, and immigration appeals. Our role is to identify the legal and evidentiary issues, develop the strongest strategy available on the facts, and advocate for our clients throughout the process.

Our Focus

Scope of Practice

Our practice is focused on Canadian immigration and citizenship matters. We do not practise refugee law and do not accept Legal Aid certificates. If you require representation in a refugee matter, we may be able to refer you to counsel practising in that area.
