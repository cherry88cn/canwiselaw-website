Legal Structure for Business Decisions

CanWise Law assists entrepreneurs, owners and businesses with the legal structure behind important commercial decisions. Clear documents and careful planning can help define responsibilities, manage risk and reduce avoidable disputes.

Advice is tailored to the transaction and the practical realities of the business—from formation and ownership arrangements to contracts, purchases, sales and ongoing corporate maintenance. Where appropriate, we coordinate with accountants and other professional advisers.
