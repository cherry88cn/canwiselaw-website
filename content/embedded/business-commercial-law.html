Buying or Selling a Business

  1. Early Discussions & Preliminary Terms

    We prepare or review non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), letters of intent (LOIs) and exclusivity arrangements, and explain the obligations they may create before a purchase agreement is signed.

  2. Legal Due Diligence

    We agree on a review scope suited to the transaction. This may include corporate records, ownership information, material contracts, commercial leases and related legal matters. Findings help inform negotiations, required consents and closing conditions.

  3. Purchase Agreement & Negotiation

    We draft, review and negotiate share or asset purchase agreements, addressing the assets or shares being acquired, the parties’ obligations, representations, warranties, closing conditions and allocation of legal risk.

  4. Closing Arrangements

    We coordinate the legal closing documents, required consents, corporate approvals and completion of agreed conditions with the parties and their advisers. The closing work follows the transaction documents and the scope of our retainer.
