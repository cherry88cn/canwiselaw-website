# Provincial overview official-source research

Research date: November 9, 2026. Research notes, not public-page copy. Sources below were opened and their substantive text read except Manitoba, whose direct retrieval returned 403 and is expressly qualified. No claim that all streams are open. No invitation figures should be treated as nominations, admissions, or a promise of selection.

## Federal framework and jurisdiction coverage

- Read: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/provincial-nominees.html (page details September 14, 2026).
- PNP jurisdictions: Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Northwest Territories, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Saskatchewan, Yukon.
- Quebec and Nunavut do not have PNPs. Quebec has separate immigration programs. Do not invent a Quebec or Nunavut PNP card.
- Provinces nominate; IRCC decides permanent residence. Express Entry aligned nomination and non-Express Entry provincial processes are separate routes. An EOI is not permanent residence or an application invitation.

## British Columbia — BC PNP

- Read: https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/about-the-bc-provincial-nominee-program/news (latest listed August 26, 2026; important change April 23).
- Read: https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/about-the-bc-provincial-nominee-program/invitations-to-apply (updated November 3, 2026).
- Current focus is Care, Build and Innovate, including priority occupations and high economic impact; Skills Immigration and entrepreneur invitations continue.
- ELSS is officially closed. No new student streams will launch. The last separate priority-technology draw was December 3, 2024; do not market old Tech invitations as a current standalone pathway. Rural/remote health support temporary registration deadline extended to October 7, 2026 and is now past.
- Suggested short wording: BC PNP 根据职业、雇主、工资及地区等因素选择申请人，目前着重护理与公共服务、建筑及高经济贡献人才。部分旧类别已关闭，毕业生并非自动具备独立申请通道。

## Alberta — AAIP

- Read: https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-processing-information (updated November 4, 2026).
- Current worker pathways include Alberta Opportunity, Rural Renewal, Tourism and Hospitality, and Alberta Express Entry; entrepreneur pathways have separate requirements.
- Worker EOI selections prioritize key sectors including technology, construction, manufacturing, aviation, agriculture and designated rural communities. Published remaining nominations are not a guarantee of available spaces for a new applicant; allocations may be redistributed.
- Suggested wording: AAIP 涵盖本省工作、乡村社区、Express Entry 及企业家等路径。员工类别采用省级意向登记和定向选择，须分别核对职业、雇主及社区要求。

## Saskatchewan — SINP

- Read: https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/sinp-processing-statistics (current 2026 figures and Q3 processing tables).
- 2026 priority sectors: health, agriculture, skilled trades, mining, manufacturing, energy, technology. Current tables list Employment Offer, Innovation and Tech Talent, Agriculture Talent, Health Talent, Existing Work Permit, International Students.
- Retail, trucking, accommodation/food-service sectors have caps and intake windows; candidates generally need six months or less remaining on work permits for those windows. Do not present generic overseas no-offer routes or all entrepreneur streams as open.
- Suggested wording: SINP 重视雇主职位和省内工作联系，并优先处理指定行业。零售、运输、住宿和餐饮等行业受名额及开放窗口限制。

## New Brunswick — NBPNP

- Read full substantive notices: https://www.gnb.ca/en/topic/family-home-community/immigration/important-notices.html (effective notices through October 14, 2026).
- NB Experience pathway in Skilled Worker limited to health, education and construction invitations since May 4. Skilled Worker and EE have NAICS 72 and specified NOC restrictions from February 3, with important employer-sector distinctions. Graduate pilot extended only in limited capacity through 2027, not a general open door for new students.
- AIP is separate: new employer designation intake paused for remainder of 2026. October 14 endorsement restriction relaxation still excludes four specified NOCs; overseas candidates remain limited to government-led recruitment initiatives in health/education/construction.
- Suggested wording: NBPNP 设有技术工人、Express Entry、法语及商业等方向；目前部分路径仅邀请指定行业，并设职业及雇主行业限制。

## Nova Scotia — NSNP

- Read: https://liveinnovascotia.com/resources/nsnp-update-four-consolidated-streams (February 18, 2026).
- Read: https://liveinnovascotia.com/resources/update-nova-scotia-nominee-program-and-atlantic-immigration-program-2026-selection (July 14, 2026).
- Four consolidated streams: Nova Scotia Graduate, Skilled Worker, Entrepreneur, Nova Scotia: Express Entry. Old ten streams should not be represented as ten current separate routes.
- EOI selection remains priority-based. July one-time expansion applies to active EOIs submitted on/before June 30 and prescribed in-province workers with work permits expiring in 2026 or earlier; it is not a blanket open invitation to new applicants.
- Suggested wording: NSNP 现整合为毕业生、技术工人、企业家及 Express Entry 四个方向。省内就业、紧缺职业及当期选择重点均影响获选机会，符合资格不等于一定获邀。

## Prince Edward Island — PEI PNP

- Read: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/office-of-immigration/expression-of-interest-draws (published October 16, 2026).
- Current priority: healthcare, trades, manufacturing and other shortage sectors. Sales/service workers may not receive invitations. 2026 table through October 16 shows labour/EE invitations and zero entrepreneur invitations; zero invitations alone is not proof that EOI submission is formally closed.
- Suggested wording: PEI PNP 按本省劳动力需要从 EOI 中选择，近期重点为医疗、技工、制造等领域。销售及服务行业目前可能无法获邀，须结合具体职业及省内就业情况评估。

## Newfoundland and Labrador — NLPNP

- Read: https://www.gov.nl.ca/immigration/invitations-to-apply-updates/ (updated November 4, 2026).
- EOI model covers economic applications; invitations are issued in batches. Latest table includes both NLPNP and AIP separately. Existence of an EOI does not imply invitation, nomination or AIP endorsement.
- Suggested wording: NLPNP 按省内就业及经济需求筛选意向登记，获邀后方进入正式申请。该省的 AIP 同样受筛选安排影响，但属于另一移民计划。

## Yukon — YNP

- Read: https://yukon.ca/en/yukon-nominee-program (updated November 2, 2026).
- Employer-driven Critical Impact Worker, Skilled Worker and Express Entry categories. Employers submit; EOI selection precedes full applications.
- Both listed 2026 EOI intakes (January 19–30 and July 6–17) are explicitly CLOSED. Earlier EOIs did not carry into July intake. Do not say currently accepting applications as a blanket claim.
- Suggested wording: YNP 以育空雇主支持为基础，按年度窗口筛选。截至核对日，2026 年两个公布的 EOI 窗口均已结束，下一轮须等待官方安排。

## Northwest Territories — NTNP

- Read: https://www.immigratenwt.ca/expression-interest (current page includes September 25, 2026 draw and next-draw note).
- Read: https://www.gov.nt.ca/en/newsroom/nwt-nominee-program-opens-updated-selection-process and https://www.gov.nt.ca/en/newsroom/nwt-nominee-programs-employer-driven-stream-expression-interest-draw-dates-released .
- Employer-Driven stream uses EOI ranking; profiles have no overall submission deadline or numeric limit. Current official EOI page says next draw in early 2027, exact date to be confirmed late2026. Francophone and Business operate separately. Do not rely on older Immigrate Here landing-page banner about 2025 closure.
- Suggested wording: NTNP 雇主驱动类别采用 EOI 排名，法语及商业类别另有流程。当前官方公告将下一次雇主驱动 EOI 抽选安排在 2027 年初；登记不等于已经获得申请名额。

## Atlantic Immigration Program — AIP (not PNP)

- Read: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration.html (September 1, 2026).
- Read: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/how-to-immigrate.html (federal page marks Open).
- Covers NB, NS, PEI, NL; designated-employer job offer required. Candidate needs applicable education/language/experience or graduate exemption, settlement arrangements and provincial endorsement, followed by federal PR application. Provincial endorsement is not PNP nomination.
- Federal Open label does not negate provincial designation pauses, occupational restrictions or endorsement selection/allocations (NB example above, NS selection priorities, NL EOI).
- Suggested wording: 大西洋移民计划（AIP）覆盖四个大西洋省份，是独立的雇主驱动移民计划，不是省提名项目。通常需要指定雇主的合资格工作邀请、省级背书及安居计划；各省受理与筛选安排仍须分别核对。

## Editorial treatment

Use a short checked-date line, provincial cards and direct official “资格与最新状态” links. Do not reproduce a long academic bibliography in the client-facing page. Present positive fit factors and concrete limitations without promising invitation, processing time or PR. Provincial EOI scores are different systems and are not interchangeable with OINP EOI or federal CRS. Do not lead the overview with physicians; healthcare is only one priority sector where supported by official evidence.
