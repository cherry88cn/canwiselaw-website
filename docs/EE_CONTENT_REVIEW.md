## What changed in CanWise's original copy

- Separate eligibility, CRS ranking, category requirements and invitation/approval.
- Provide a compact, expressly non-exhaustive CEC/FSW/FST comparison; identify the separate FSW selection grid.
- Explain assessment, profile maintenance, invitation review and full application as distinct stages.
- Group evidence by stage and explain actual duties, work periods and consistency concerns.
- State concrete legal services without inventing products, fees, results or timelines.
- Address offers, high-wage versus category lists, temporary status and waiting time in short FAQs.
- Keep dated reform guidance, historical invitation records and the calculator visibly distinct.

No competitor passage, scoring table, fee, success rate, cutoff prediction or guarantee was copied. Outdated 1,200-point ceilings, removed-offer-point claims, 50/200 offer tables, 90-day invitation deadlines and old fund amounts were rejected.
