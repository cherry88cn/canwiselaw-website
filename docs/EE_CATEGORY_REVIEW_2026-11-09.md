# EE category and FAQ preview review

Reviewed November 9, 2026. Local preview only; not a publication record.

## Controlling sources

- [IRCC category criteria and NOC lists](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html): ten current categories; eight occupational categories require 12 months in one listed occupation in the past three years, not necessarily continuous. Physicians, senior managers and researchers require Canadian experience. French requires NCLC 7 in all four abilities. Military applicants have separate requirements. Verified DOM IDs `result-1` through `result-10` correspond to the displayed order.
- [IRCC job offers](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/job-offer.html), [high-wage list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/high-wage.html), and [current Ministerial Instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/mandate/policies-operational-instructions-agreements/ministerial-instructions/express-entry-application-management-system/current.html), sections 15–15.2, control the FAQ corrections. Occupation tier is not an individual salary test; offer and Canadian-work paths are compared, not added.

## Resulting copy

Original bilingual wording: short overview, shared experience conditions, ten linked category descriptions, and separation from high-wage scoring. No guaranteed draw dates, cutoff forecasts or competitor quotations. The current official page controls conflicts even when a third-party page has a newer-looking review label.

The user-provided five-page PDF supplied the job-offer question themes. No PDF or client material is stored here. Corrections avoid treating personal wages as occupation tiers, LMIA removal as automatic eligibility, offer points as additive, or optional employer evidence as universally mandatory. The first question explicitly includes 1.3/1.5/2 times. Chinese and English use the same five questions and substantive answers.
