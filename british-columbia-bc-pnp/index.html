Work experienceUp to 40 points

Qualifying directly related experience in the last 10 years; part-time duration counts at 50%.

What counts as directly related experience?

Normally paid work in the same NOC. Related work at an equal or higher TEER requires evidence of direct relevance; related TEER 1 experience may also count for a TEER 0 offer. The stream’s two-year skilled-work requirement is separate from directly related experience scored here.

Study-permit work is generally excluded; full-time paid skilled co-op may qualify after graduation. Doctoral research/coursework may qualify under section 3.8 even if unpaid, but does not automatically earn the Canadian-experience bonus. Select “Needs review” if uncertain.

Which designations qualify?

Corresponding trade certificates (including Red Seal certificates from other provinces) or SkilledTradesBC registered apprenticeships; and the specified BC registration/licence for the occupations below.

  • Dental hygienists · 32111

  • Health care aides · 33102
  • Pharmacy technicians · 32124
  • Practical nurses · 32101
  • TCM practitioners and acupuncturists · 32200
  • Veterinary technicians · 32104

LanguageUp to 40 points

Use the lowest of four skills in one test; use the stronger valid test if you have both languages. Results must be less than two years old at registration.

Convert test scores to CLB / NCLC

Conversion for valid results only; this does not verify validity or bilingual eligibility. For TEF use Équivalence ancien score, not Score / 699.

All four skills in both valid tests must reach level 4. Speaking two languages alone is not enough.

Job-offer wageUp to 55 points

Exclude bonuses, commissions, tips, overtime and allowances. A scoring band does not establish wage eligibility.

Convert an annual salary to hourly pay

Annual salary ÷ 52 ÷ weekly hours. Non-teacher hours must be 30–40. The unrounded result determines the points band.

Work locationUp to 25 points

Use the main physical workplace; home-based work uses your residence, where duties must reasonably be performed.

Pathways and application process

Skilled Worker requires an eligible BC job offer and employer support, with occupation, experience, language and income requirements. Health Authority applicants apply directly without this registration score. EEBC also requires eligibility for a federal Express Entry program. Entrepreneur Immigration uses a separate assessment system.

  1. Check applicant, job and employer eligibility

    A TEER 0–3 offer must still be checked against ineligible occupations. A full-time indeterminate offer is normally required; listed occupations may qualify with a limited-term offer.

  2. Register and wait for an invitation

    Skilled Worker registrations remain active for up to 12 months. Selection may depend on occupation, region, wage or points, rather than the total alone.

  3. Apply after invitation, then proceed federally after nomination

    Submit the complete nomination application within 30 calendar days after invitation. After nomination, apply to IRCC for permanent residence.
